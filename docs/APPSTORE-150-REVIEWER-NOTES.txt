Changes and test paths:
1. Notifications: open the notification bell, then Notification preferences. Set campaign push/email preferences, time zone, and quiet hours. Campaign email requires explicit opt-in. 2. Announcements: banner dismissal is saved to the account. Promotional banners stay off rehearsal, recording, and review screens. The staff Communications Center supports audience previews and scheduling; it is intentionally unavailable to the ordinary actor demo account.
3. Practice: open Scene Study, select a script and character, and start rehearsal. The revised layout puts the script and listening controls first, with a visible Finish action after scene completion.
4. Review: open Tape Review. Completed notes have a compact summary and expandable detail. Reopening saved notes preserves access without starting another analysis. Review-completion tracking no longer counts reopening saved notes as a new review.
5. Profile: Actors Access and Casting Networks fields save public profile links and provide guidance. These are bookmarks, not audition-inbox synchronization or third-party login integrations.
6. Navigation and update notices: the mobile studio shell and tablet update banner have been refined. Included-AI-action limit copy is clearer; allowances are unchanged.
