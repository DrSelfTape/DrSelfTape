# Actor Needs — Macro Research Brief (2026-06-09)

*Source: 9-agent web swarm across Reddit, Deadline, Variety, Hollywood Reporter, Backstage, SAG-AFTRA, Stage 32, Bloomberg Law, app-store reviews. 728K tokens, 282 web lookups. Pain points ranked by cross-source frequency + emotional intensity.*

## The headline

**DST has quietly built nearly the entire actor craft-and-survival stack — but markets only "Unlimited AI," so its biggest wins are invisible and its three sharpest, defensible differentiators are under-built or unspoken.**

This is a **positioning problem, not a feature-gap problem.** The swarm kept finding that the thing actors are begging for, DST already half-ships — it's just buried.

> ⚠️ **Caveat — ground-truth the "already have" claims first.** The DST feature inventory used for this mapping came from the app-context I supplied plus an agent's inference, *not* a fresh repo read. Some claimed features (EvolutionMetric, ReaderReview, Stripe-Connect reader payouts, a paid reader marketplace) need verifying against the real codebase before we act on "just market what you have." If a feature is named here that doesn't actually exist, its recommendation flips from "position it" to "build it."

## Top 3 highest-leverage moves

1. **Own the no-reader problem end-to-end as the brand's spine.** Fuse DST's reader assets (AI scene partner + free Green Room matching + any paid booking) into ONE "find a reader in 30 seconds, any hour" surface, and invest in **pause-aware/cue-recognition timing** so the AI reader beats the timer-based competitors actors openly distrust. #1 pain; DST may be the only player holding all three answers.
2. **Reposition pricing as the explicit anti-pay-to-play alternative.** While Actors Access & Casting Networks face 2024 class-actions for per-submission fees, DST should publicly guarantee **no per-tape/per-submission/per-upload charges**, add an annual/one-time option to escape subscription fatigue, and frame tokens as "AI compute only, never gatekeeping." The market is handing DST a trust wedge for free.
3. **Close the audition black hole with a feedback + outcome loop.** Surface CDSim / CastingDirectorAI structured notes as "the feedback you'll never get from real casting," and turn audition-tracker booked/callback status into a personal win-rate / "am I in the ballpark?" dashboard.

## The strategic punchline

Actors are drowning in three things: **a labor burden** (self-tape became an unpaid shoot), **a trust crisis** (pay-to-play platforms, scams, buggy apps, AI likeness theft), and **a feedback vacuum** (submit into silence, self-direct blind). DST is positioned to answer all three — but it's selling "Unlimited AI" instead of "the actor's corner: a reader whenever you need one, honest pricing, and the feedback nobody else gives you." The work is largely **repackaging + three real builds** (reader-timing, reader-trust, outcome loop), not a ground-up roadmap.
