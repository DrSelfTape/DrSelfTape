1.3 Device Permissions

The Service may request access to the following device capabilities:

  • Camera — To record self-tape audition videos and participate in live video rehearsals
  • Microphone — To capture audio during self-tape recording, live scene study, and video rehearsals
  • Push Notifications — To notify you of new matches, messages, and session updates
  • Screen Orientation — To lock portrait mode during recording for optimal video quality

You may grant or revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings. Denying camera or microphone access will prevent you from using recording and video call features.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide core features: self-tape recording, AI acting coach, scene study, script formatting, and scene generation
  • Match you with other actors for reading and rehearsal through the Green Room
  • Process payments for paid reader sessions via Stripe
  • Generate AI-powered feedback on your performances using third-party AI services
  • Provide text-to-speech (TTS) for scene partner dialogue during practice
  • Track your auditions, submissions, and acting progress
  • Send push notifications about matches, messages, and updates
  • Improve the Service through usage analytics
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform safety
  • Respond to your support requests

3. AI Services & Data Processing

Our Service uses third-party artificial intelligence (AI) services to power features like the Acting Coach, Scene Generator, Script Formatter, and Scene Partner. When you use these features:

  • What is sent: Script text, scene dialogue, and your performance context are sent to our AI providers to generate feedback, scenes, or formatted scripts. Your video recordings are never sent to AI providers.
  • Providers: We use Anthropic (Claude) as our primary AI provider and OpenAI as a backup. We also use ElevenLabs for text-to-speech voice synthesis.
  • Data retention by AI providers: Our AI providers process data via their commercial APIs. Per their API terms, input data is not used to train their models and is not retained beyond the request lifecycle. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy and OpenAI's API Data Policy.
  • Audio transcription: If you use voice features, audio may be transcribed using OpenAI's Whisper service. Audio data is processed in real-time and not stored by the provider.

5. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS/SSL (HTTPS)
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and never stored in plain text
  • Authentication uses JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with configurable expiration
  • Payment data is handled entirely by Stripe (PCI DSS Level 1 compliant) — we never access or store your full card number
  • Self-tape video recordings are stored in encrypted cloud storage (Amazon S3)
  • Database access is restricted and monitored

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days.
  • Self-tape recordings: Stored until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Chat messages: Retained for as long as the associated match exists.
  • Journal entries: Retained until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Payment records: Transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years as required for tax and legal compliance.
  • Server logs: Automatically purged after 90 days.

  • Restriction: You may request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  • Objection: You may object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdrawal of consent: You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

7.3 California Residents — CCPA/CPRA

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act provides you with additional rights:
